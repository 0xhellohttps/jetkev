Pollution Control Unit

PCU

JetKev is your very own Pollution Control Unit (PCU), crafted to ensure a cleaner, cooler, and more enjoyable grilling experience.

Imagine enjoying your BBQ without the heavy smoke lingering in the air or overwhelming your outdoor space.

02
How It Works

Function

The Grill door is in communication with the flue. When the Grill door is opened the flue is opened and the smoke is drawn into the duct.

03
Wool & Carbon

Filtration

JetKev is a 3 stage filtration system using wool and carbon filters to capture the smoke.

Wool is a Grease magnet that is noncombustible and biodegradable.
